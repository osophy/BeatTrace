# BeatTrace small-user test plan

## Purpose and scope

Run a formative session with fewer than 10 dancers. Recruit beginner, intermediate, and advanced freestyle dancers. This is a plan and observation form; no participant results have been collected or inferred.

## Session outline (30–45 minutes)

1. Explain local-file privacy and that beat, section, layer, and pitch cues are estimates. Ask participants to use a song they may legally provide.
2. Ask the dancer to load a file, play/pause, set music volume, and identify the first beat without assistance.
3. Try 4-beat and 8-beat patterns, the count guide, tempo adjustment, tap-to-align, and beat nudges.
4. Explore the full-song overview, section cues, estimated upcoming events, and Advanced-only moving timeline. Check that Beginner and Intermediate do not display the moving timeline.
5. In Intermediate Mode, compare the original mix with layer practice; ask the dancer to use visibility, solo, mute, and volume controls. Explicitly state filters are approximate.
6. If the optional local model is available, ask permission before they choose its local analysis button; observe progress, cancellation, and fallback. Do not upload to cloud.
7. Ask what was confusing, distracting, useful during practice, and what they would change.
